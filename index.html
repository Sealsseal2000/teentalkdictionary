About the project

Why do we switch languages?

Code-switching happens when a speaker alternates between two or more languages during a conversation or utterance.

↔

What is code-switching?

Code-switching is the use of more than one language in the same conversation. Speakers may switch languages to express an idea, react naturally, or communicate with friends.

Aa

Word-level switching

A word from another language is inserted into a sentence, for example: “Я забыла homework.”

“ ”

Phrase-level switching

A phrase from another language appears within a conversation, for example: “Всё, let's go!”

¶

Sentence-level switching

The speaker changes language between clauses or sentences, for example: “Мен шаршадым. I need some rest.”

The examples on this site are for learning and demonstration. The frequency and form of code-switching can vary by person, context and region. Use your own survey results to make evidence-based claims.
